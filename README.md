# BrandGuard AI

### AI-Powered Advertisement Verification and Brand Governance Platform

BrandGuard AI is an intelligent platform designed to help businesses evaluate advertisements, identify misleading or potentially non-compliant claims, and maintain consistent brand communication.

## Problem Statement

In today's digital world, advertisements can contain misleading information, exaggerated claims, unsupported promises, and inconsistent brand messaging. This can create false expectations among customers and damage consumer trust.

Businesses need a reliable way to review advertising content before it reaches their audience.

## Proposed Solution

BrandGuard AI aims to provide an AI-powered advertisement verification and brand governance workflow that:

- Analyses advertisement content.
- Identifies potentially misleading or unsupported claims.
- Detects inconsistencies with predefined brand guidelines.
- Explains detected issues.
- Suggests appropriate corrections.
- Supports human review and approval.
- Maintains revision history and governance insights.

## Key Features (Planned)

- AI-powered advertisement analysis.
- Brand guideline management.
- Claim and content verification.
- Issue detection and explanations.
- AI-generated correction suggestions.
- Human-in-the-loop approval.
- Review history and governance dashboard.
